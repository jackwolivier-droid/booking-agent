# FAQ (SYNTHETIC)

**Do you take walk-ins?** No, it's a solo studio and runs by appointment only.

**Can I bring a design / inspo photo?** Yes, please send it when you book so the
right amount of time is set aside.

**How long does a full set take?** About 90 minutes.

**Do you do removals of work from another salon?** Yes, book "gel removal +
manicure" and mention it was done elsewhere.

**Do you offer hair, lashes, or waxing?** No — nails only.
