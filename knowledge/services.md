Polish & Co is a solo nail studio offering:

- **Gel manicure** — long-wearing gel polish on natural nails. ~45 min.
- **Gel removal + manicure** — safe soak-off of old gel, then a fresh gel manicure. ~60 min.
- **Full set (acrylic)** — acrylic extensions, shaped and polished. ~90 min.
- **Fill / refill** — maintenance on an existing acrylic set. ~60 min.
- **Classic manicure** — shape, cuticle care, regular polish. ~30 min.
- **Pedicure** — soak, exfoliation, nail and cuticle care, polish. ~50 min.

We do not currently offer hair, waxing, or lash services.
